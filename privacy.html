Privacy Policy

App: Arrows Puzzle
Developer: GoDxWraith
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrows Puzzle ("the app", "the game") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information directly.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (current level, hints, settings, daily missions and statistics) only on your own device using local storage. This data is not sent to us and can be removed by using "Reset progress" in Settings or by clearing the app data.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by third-party advertising networks (such as Adsterra). These networks may use cookies, device identifiers, IP address and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance. We do not control the data collected by these networks. Please read their own privacy policies for details.

The game also contains a "Support the developer" link that opens a sponsored page in your browser.

4. Children

The app is a general puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Security

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store it on our servers. Your game progress stays on your device.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
